## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and operators of established, owner-run businesses in the USA (trades, manufacturing, wholesale and distribution, retail, professional services). They already have customers, a team and tools that work, but revenue leaks through slow quoting, missed follow-ups, manual entry and data nobody acts on. They visit to judge, in a few minutes, whether Vorcove can find that lost revenue and whether these are people they can trust to build on top of their setup. Their job on the site: understand the offer, see proof it works, and request a business review.

## Product Purpose
Vorcove is a software and AI product studio. It builds systems on top of a business's existing tools, processes and data to grow revenue, without replacing anything. Every engagement starts with a business review that maps where revenue is lost or capped. Success for the client is measured in revenue, not features.

## Positioning
Builds on top of what the business already runs (no rip-and-replace), and measures success by revenue. The client owns all code, data and IP. Clients work directly with the people building the system. Vorcove also builds its own vertical products on the same idea, which double as proof of how it works.

## Operating Context
Services: Automation, AI agents, Data & revenue insights, Custom software.
Engagement: business review → plan (fixes ranked by revenue impact, project scoped from it) → build on top of existing tools for a one-time build fee, first working version in about two weeks → measure and improve, with an optional monthly plan for ongoing support.
Enquiries come through the site's contact form (business name, email, goals); Vorcove replies within 24 hours. Contact email: hello@vorcove.com.
Products:
- EventSmith: AI quoting for event rental businesses. Reads each inquiry, builds the quote from the business's own catalog and live stock, waits for approval, follows up. Live demo at https://eventsmith.vercel.app.
- Leadsmith: enquiry-to-quote and follow-up for metal fabricators. Not built yet; a demo walkthrough exists. Do not describe it as live, in pilot, or coming soon.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Existing codebase: React (Vite) client with an Express + MongoDB API for contact leads; deployed on Vercel.
- Nothing is offered for free. Never say "small" businesses.
- No UK-only framing; the target market is the USA (US spelling, US conventions).
- Prefer "systems" over "software" in headlines; mention revenue.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Vorcove. Vorcove brand leads; products get supporting mention.
- Logo (the "Harbor Beacon" mark: a disc with a V-shaped cove cut in and a beacon dot) is final.
- Coloring is final: deep sea-ink grounds and the beacon orange accent (see client/src/index.css tokens).
- Voice: plain, confident, short. No hype or "AI magic" language.
- Founders (Usman, CEO; Umer, CTO) are not featured on the site for now.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work, don't claim it: demos and the review process are the proof.
2. The client's existing setup is respected, never replaced.
3. Revenue is the unit of value in every message.
4. One clear next step: the business review.
