# Homepage (client/src/App.jsx)

Scope: the one-page Vorcove marketing site. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: owners of established US businesses with working tools and leaking revenue. Action: book a business review (the contact form). Proof: the EventSmith and Leadsmith walkthrough videos, the EventSmith live demo, and the review process itself. No client results exist; sample figures are labeled illustrative.
Constraints: logo and colors fixed; founders not shown; contact form fields, validation and API unchanged.
Memorable moment: ticking fixes in the sample review ledger and watching the recovered total recompute.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the ledger from a Vorcove business review: revenue leaks as line items, ranked by dollars, totaled in beacon orange. It refuses the dark video hero, headline-over-cards and stat-counter template.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white ruled paper, sea-ink type and heavy 2px ink rules, hairline column rules, tabular figures, double-underlined totals. Beacon orange is the auditor's pen and is reserved for money (leaks circled, totals, recovered amounts). Sea-ink bands are the ledger's binding (header, products, footer). Archivo (wide for account heads, condensed for headlines) and Public Sans for text. Square corners, 4px max.

STORY: The visitor sees their own leaks as line items, understands that Vorcove finds and fixes them on top of existing tools, sees two products doing it, reads the terms (one-time build fee, own everything), and opens a review.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink binding bar with logo, links and the review button.
